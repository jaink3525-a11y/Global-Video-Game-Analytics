🎮 Global Gaming Market Analysis Dashboard

A interactive Power BI dashboard providing deep analytical insights into global video game sales across regions, genres, platforms, publishers, and release timelines.
 📌 Executive Summary

This project analyzes the video game industry's historical performance, market trends, regional sales breakdowns, and strategic opportunities. The dashboard transforms raw market data into actionable visual insights for game publishers, developers, and market analysts.

 📊 Key Pages & Features

The Power BI report consists of three main analytical pages:

 1. Global Gaming Market Overview
* KPI Cards: Top-level KPIs summarizing total games, sales volume, publishers, platforms, and genres.
* Global Sales by Genre: Treemap highlighting market share by genre (Action, Sports, Shooter, Racing, Role-Playing leading the market).
* Top Titles Performance: Horizontal bar chart listing top-selling titles (e.g., FIFA 15, FIFA 14, Madden NFL 08, Monopoly, LEGO Star Wars II).
* Historical Trend Line: Timeline chart illustrating global sales trajectories from 1980 to 2020, highlighting peak industry growth between 2005–2010.
* Regional Sales Split: Donut chart showcasing North America (NA), PAL regions (Europe/Africa), Japan (JP), and Other sales distribution.

 2. Platform, Genre & Game Performance
* Console Sales Distribution: Analysis of total sales performance across hardware systems.
* Genre Breakdown: Total sales volume across Action, Sports, Misc, Adventure, Shooter, and RPGs.
* Critic Score Correlation: Scatter chart showing the relationship between critic review scores and total sales revenue across genres.
* Publisher Leaderboards: Sales volume rankings across major publishers.

 3. Regional Market & Strategic Opportunities
* Regional Metrics: KPI breakdown across Japan ($688M), PAL Region ($2B), North America ($3B), and Other regions ($651M).
* Regional Genre Comparison: Clustered bar chart illustrating regional preferences (e.g., Action/Shooter dominating NA/PAL vs. RPGs showing strong relative strength in Japan).
* Matrix Heatmap: Detailed regional and genre cross-analysis for deep data drill-down.
* Regional Timeline: Comparative line graphs tracking sales evolution across regions over four decades.


## 💡 Key Business Insights
1. Dominant Regions: North America and PAL (Europe) represent the vast majority of total market revenue, accounting for over ~70% of global sales combined.
2. Top Performing Genres: Action, Sports, and Shooter consistently generate the highest overall sales volume globally.
3. Historical Peak: Industry sales peaked around 2008–2009, driven by Seventh Generation consoles (Xbox 360, PS3, Nintendo Wii).
4. Critic Score Impact: High critic scores (8.5+) strongly correlate with multi-million-unit sales hits, particularly in the Shooter and Action genres.
